# Venue detail

Presentation rules for `/venues/[id]`:

## Hero (tour)
- One muted looping clip via `HeroMedia` + ambient + navy scrim
- Brand company eyebrow, venue name (display), location / hours / rating
- Primary CTA: **Book a court** scrolls to courts (calendar lives on `/courts/[id]`)

## Below hero
- Quick facts strip (courts, from price, sports count, hours) — flat cards, no shader
- Sport chips
- **Gallery** — static photos only (tour video stays in hero; never two autoplay loops)
- **Courts & booking** — hover preview video on cards; accent “Book slots” → court calendar

## Mobile
- Sticky bottom CTA to first court with “from PKR” — above player tab bar
